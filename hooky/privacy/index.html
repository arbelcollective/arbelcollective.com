Hooky Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 October 2026 · Applies to the Hooky app on Android and iOS

Hooky is a market trading game made by Arbel Collective, based in the United Kingdom. In short: Hooky does not ask for an account, and we (Arbel Collective) do not collect personal information about you. Hooky does show optional adverts provided by Google AdMob, which uses some information about your device, as explained below. This page explains the small amount of information the game uses.

Information saved on your device

To make the game work, Hooky saves some information only on your own phone or tablet: your settings (sound, music, vibration, hints), your best scores, whether you have played today's Daily Challenge, and any game you are part-way through. This stays on your device and is not sent to us. Uninstalling Hooky deletes it.

Leaderboards

Hooky's worldwide leaderboards are run by Google Play Games Services. If you choose to use them, your Play Games profile name and avatar and the scores you post are shared with Google and shown to other players on the leaderboard. This is handled by Google under the Google Privacy Policy, and you can manage or hide your Play Games profile in the Google Play Games settings. We can see the scores posted to Hooky's leaderboards, but we do not receive your email address or any other contact details.

Purchases

Hooky may offer optional in-app purchases. Payments are handled entirely by Google Play or the Apple App Store, under their own privacy policies; we never see your card or payment details. When purchases are switched on, we use RevenueCat, a purchase-management service, to confirm what you have bought. It receives an anonymous app user ID and your purchase history, but no name, email or payment details.

Adverts

Hooky shows adverts only when you choose to watch one, for example to get five extra trading days at the end of a city. There are no pop-up or banner adverts. These "rewarded" adverts are provided by Google AdMob.

To show an advert, measure it and prevent fraud, AdMob may collect and use information such as your device's advertising ID, your IP address and approximate location, the type of device and operating system you use, and how you interact with the advert. This is handled by Google under the Google Privacy Policy; you can read more in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Your choices: if you are in the UK, the EU or another region where consent is required, Hooky asks for your consent before personalised adverts are shown, using Google's consent message. You can change your choice at any time from the game's settings. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised adverts, in your phone's settings (on Android: Settings → Google → Ads; on iPhone: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking). Watching adverts is always optional, and you can play the whole game without watching any.

Children

Hooky is designed for players aged 13 and over and is not aimed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Your rights

Because we do not hold personal information about you, there is normally nothing for us to access, correct or delete. To remove your scores from Hooky's leaderboards or your purchase record from RevenueCat, or for any other privacy question, contact us and we will help. Information used by Google for adverts is managed through your Google account and phone settings, described above. If you are in the UK or EU you also have the right to complain to your data protection authority (in the UK, the Information Commissioner's Office).

How to delete your Hooky data

  1. Data on your phone (settings, scores, games in progress): uninstall Hooky, or clear its storage in your phone's Settings → Apps → Hooky. It is deleted straight away.
  2. Advert data: reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings (on Android: Settings → Google → Ads).
  3. Leaderboard scores or your purchase record: email arbelcollective@gmail.com with the subject "Hooky: delete my data". We delete it within 30 days and email you to confirm.

Changes to this policy

If Hooky changes the way it uses information, we will update this page and the date at the top.
