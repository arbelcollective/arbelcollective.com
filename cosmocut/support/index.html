Cosmocut help

Something not working, or got an idea? We read every message.

Contact us

Email arbelcollective@gmail.com and we'll reply as soon as we can.

To help us sort it quickly, please include:

  • what happened, and what you expected to happen
  • your phone model
  • which mode, system or sector you were on

Feedback from the game

You can also tap Send feedback on Cosmocut's home screen or pause screen. It opens your email app with your note ready to send.

Adverts and your choices

Cosmocut only shows an advert when you choose to watch one, for example for an Emergency Relaunch after a crash. You can change your advert choices in the game under Settings → Privacy and ads.

Your data

Cosmocut keeps your game on your phone. To see what little leaves it, or how to ask for it to be deleted, read the privacy policy.
