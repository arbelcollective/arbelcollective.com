Cosmocut Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 November 2026 · Applies to the Cosmocut app on Android

Cosmocut is a space game made by Arbel Collective, based in the United Kingdom. In short: Cosmocut does not ask for an account, and we (Arbel Collective) do not collect personal information about you. Cosmocut does show optional adverts provided by Google AdMob, which uses some information about your device, as explained below.

Information saved on your device

To make the game work, Cosmocut saves some information only on your own phone or tablet: your settings (sound, music, vibration, controls), your best scores, your Outer Reaches progress, ships and Stardust, and whether you have played today's Daily Challenge. This stays on your device and is not sent to us. Uninstalling Cosmocut deletes it.

Leaderboards

Cosmocut's worldwide leaderboards are run by Google Play Games Services. If you choose to use them, your Play Games profile name and avatar and the scores you post are shared with Google and shown to other players. This is handled by Google under the Google Privacy Policy. We can see the scores posted to Cosmocut's leaderboards, but we do not receive your email address or any other contact details.

Adverts

Cosmocut shows an advert only when you choose to watch one, for example for an Emergency Relaunch after a crash. There are no pop-up or banner adverts. These "rewarded" adverts are provided by Google AdMob.

To show an advert, measure it and prevent fraud, AdMob may collect and use information such as your device's advertising ID, your IP address and approximate location, the type of device and operating system you use, and how you interact with the advert. This is handled by Google under the Google Privacy Policy; see also How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Your choices: if you are in the UK, the EU or another region where consent is required, Cosmocut asks for your consent before personalised adverts are shown, using Google's consent message. You can change your choice at any time from Settings → Privacy and ads. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised adverts, in your phone's settings (on Android: Settings → Google → Ads). Watching adverts is always optional, and you can play the whole game without watching any.

Feedback

If you send feedback from the game, it opens your own email app with a message to us. We only receive what you choose to send, and we use it only to reply and improve the game.

Children

Cosmocut is designed for players aged 13 and over and is not aimed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Your rights

Because we do not hold personal information about you, there is normally nothing for us to access, correct or delete. To remove your scores from Cosmocut's leaderboards, or for any other privacy question, contact us and we will help. If you are in the UK or EU you also have the right to complain to your data protection authority (in the UK, the Information Commissioner's Office).

Changes to this policy

If Cosmocut changes the way it uses information, we will update this page and the date at the top.
